## Product scope

Rangoon is designed to manage agent capabilities such as instructions, skills, workflows, tools, connectors, policies, tests, versions, deployment targets, and evidence. It is designed around LNSAT for execution authorization and evidence. This public website is a static product site; it does not expose a callable agent runtime, MCP server, A2A endpoint, or production authorization API.

Screenshots and metrics on this site are design previews and sample data. They are not customer, production, performance, availability, compatibility, or certification evidence.
